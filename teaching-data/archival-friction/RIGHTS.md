## What this repository adds

The source-record selection, transcription, synthetic perturbations,
decision log, evaluation data, descriptions, and instructional prose were
created for this handbook. Handbook text and original annotations are
released under CC BY 4.0; original code is released under MIT, following the
repository licences.

The synthetic rows and field changes are not alterations to the source PDF.
They are isolated in a declared CSV and are labelled in every derived table.

## Reuse cautions

- Preserve the source citation and public-domain provenance when you copy
  the facsimile.
- Do not present the provider OCR as a diplomatic transcription.
- Do not infer the identity of “Mr. Meker” from a spelling resemblance
  alone. The packet leaves this authority match unresolved.
- Historical portraits and polemical captions require contextual reading
  even when copyright restrictions have expired.
- No third-party screenshot is included. The committed PDF is the archival
  facsimile made available by the named providers.
