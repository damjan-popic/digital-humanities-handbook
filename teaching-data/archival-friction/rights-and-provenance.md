## Reuse checklist

- Cite the dLib record and the historical issue; identify Commons as the PDF
  download and public-domain assessment page.
- Preserve the source PDF and exact dLib TXT-export digests; distinguish the
  normalized provider excerpt from the handbook reference transcription.
- Do not imply that CC BY creates new rights in faithfully transcribed
  underlying public-domain text.
- Attribute original handbook selection, annotations, descriptions and
  documentation under CC BY 4.0, and original code under MIT.
- Do not present synthetic teaching disturbances as archival evidence.
- Recheck provider availability, interfaces and rights statements when the
  packet is redistributed; they are mutable. The publisher's v1.0 rights
  review must resolve cross-jurisdiction clearance, including the visible
  Commons notice about a missing United States public-domain tag.
